\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Spiking world models forget because their neurons are reset at every environment step and at every spike, which leaves their time constants nothing to act on.
\method keeps memory in persistent, normalized integrator and resonant dendrites that no spike touches, and communicates through the spikes of a stateless soma.
A spiking world model built from these neurons remembers further back than DreamerV3 on BSuite and recalls exact lags that DreamerV3 cannot, at a recurrent firing rate of 0.15 and 3.9$\times$ less arithmetic energy per step.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
The dendritic state is graded, so deployment needs neuromorphic cores with multi-variable neurons and layer normalization, and our energy estimate counts arithmetic but not memory access.
